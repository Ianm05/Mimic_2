How to enroll

Gather your documents, book your intake appointment, and start training. Here's everything you need to bring and what happens next.

What to bring

Bring all four of these to your intake appointment.

  1. A valid photo ID

    Any one of these. It must not be expired.

    • U.S. passport
    • Green card (Permanent Resident Card)
    • Foreign passport
    • State-issued ID or driver's license

  2. Your Social Security card

    Bring the card itself, not just the number.

  3. Two reference letters

    Letters from people who know your work or character, such as a former employer, supervisor, or teacher.

    References from family members or friends are not accepted.

  4. A physical exam with lab results

    A completed physical exam from your doctor, including your lab results.

How it works

Four steps from your first call to your first class.

  1. Contact us

    Call us or fill out an application form to tell us which program you're interested in.

  2. Gather your documents

    Collect your ID, Social Security card, two reference letters, and physical exam results.

  3. Intake appointment

    Meet with our team, hand in your documents, and choose your class schedule.

  4. Start training

    Begin classes in English or Spanish.

Common questions

My ID is from another country. Can I still apply?

Yes. A valid foreign passport is accepted as your photo ID.

Who can write my reference letters?

Anyone who knows your work or character and isn't family or a friend, such as a former employer, supervisor, or teacher.

Are classes taught in Spanish?

Yes. Training is offered in English or Spanish.

How do I pay for the program?

We offer weekly payment plans. Call us to talk through your options.

Ready to start?

Choose your program, or call us and we'll help you decide.

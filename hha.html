How it works

Four steps from application to your first patient.

  1. Apply

    Fill out the short form below to tell us about yourself.

  2. Interview and screening

    A brief interview, background check, and medical clearance.

  3. Training and exam

    State-approved classes and a hands-on skills test.

  4. Start working

    Get your NYS certificate and your first patients in the borough you choose.

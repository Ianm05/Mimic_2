Who we are

The Dennelisse LHCSA is a NYS DOE approved continuing Education provider for Social Workers. With a vast experience in Social Services since its incepetion in 1987. The organization has sought to improve the skills and talent with the changing needs of the communties we serve. In doing so, we collaborate and share our expertise with clinical and nonclinical professionals for knowledge sharing compliance, and best practices. Some of the courses offered include challenges while working with substance abuse and declining mental health,changing demographics, and Health care disparities. Contunuing educatuion courses for social Workers are offered throughout the year.

Our mission statement

The Dennelisse LHCSA corporation seeks to improve the health and well-being of disenfranchised children,families and individuals in all communities, while mepowering them with educational and healthcare resources to achieve their full potential..

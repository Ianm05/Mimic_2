Who we are

The Dennelisse LHCSA is a NYSED-approved continuing education provider for social workers. With experience in social services since our founding in 1987, we work to strengthen the skills of professionals as the needs of the communities we serve change. We collaborate with clinical and nonclinical professionals to share expertise on compliance and best practices. Our courses cover topics such as substance abuse, declining mental health, changing demographics, and healthcare disparities, and are offered throughout the year.

Our mission

The Dennelisse LHCSA seeks to improve the health and well-being of disenfranchised children, families, and individuals in all communities, while empowering them with educational and healthcare resources to reach their full potential.
